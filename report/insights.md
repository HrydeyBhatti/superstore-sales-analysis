#Superstore sales analysis
- Dataset obtained from Kaggle - samplesuperstore.csv
- Author of project: Hrydey Bhatti

#Dataset Overview
- Contains over 10,000 retail sales records across mulitple regions, product categories, and customer segments

- Includes information such as sales, proft, discount, quantity, and shipping details


#Initial Observations

- Dataset contains sales and profit data for multiple product categories
- Discounts included, may reduce profit
- Shipping dates are included which shows potential for time-based analysis

#Business Questions

The data analysis will focus on the following questions:

1.  Which region generates the highest profit?
2. Which product categories are most and least profitable?
3. Which products have the most impact on losess?
4. How does discount impact profit?
5. What are the monthly sales trends?
